History with context. One readable trend, conditional averages and a route back to the source table. No decorative chart animation.

Motion that explains

120ms press feedback · 220ms sheet entrance · a short checkmark after save acknowledgement. Numbers render their exact final value immediately.

Graphics that support the glass

Static chip and card line art, soft emerald light and restrained frosted layers. Reuse the existing SVG wordmark. No heavy assets or animation libraries.

Safety remains visible

44–48px controls, labels beyond colour, stable payment rows and a persistent save status. OS reduced-motion is honoured; use the toggle to preview it.
