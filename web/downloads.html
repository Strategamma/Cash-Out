Android

  1. Open Cash Out in Chrome.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu.
  3. Follow the browser prompts, then open Cash Out from its new icon.
Open Cash Out

No APK needed. Open the app online once before using it offline.

Keep a recovery copy

Records stay in this browser or installed web app. Do not assume separate browsers or installations share their records. Export a full backup in Settings and restore it after switching. Cloud setup is pending; do not rely on the device copy alone.
